Typography Principles

Use plain, simple language — no technical or accounting jargon 

Text must be easy to read and understand quickly (supports fast interactions under 10 seconds) 

Prioritize clarity over decoration — no complex or decorative text styles 


Content Guidelines

Labels and messages must be:

Clear

Direct

Actionable



Examples from PRD:

“Add Your First Expense”

“No expenses yet — tap + to add one.”

“No results found. Try a different keyword or filter.”

“No expenses in this date range to report on.” 


Feedback & Messaging

Every action must provide visible text feedback

Success confirmation after saving

Clear error messages when something fails 



Input & Validation Text

Real-time validation messages must be:

Inline

Clear and specific


No ambiguous or hidden errors
